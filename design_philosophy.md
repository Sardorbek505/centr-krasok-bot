# Digital Pigment

A philosophy where code and color dissolve into one another — where the precision of algorithms meets the visceral beauty of raw paint.

## Form and Space
Structure breathes through tension between hard geometric grids and organic chromatic explosions. Rigid dark architecture holds luminous paint-burst forms: the machine and the medium, inseparable. Space is not empty — it is pressurized, ready to spill.

## Color and Material
Deep midnight backgrounds. Electric accent strokes of cyan, magenta, gold — the colors a painter reaches for instinctively. Gradient washes that feel hand-mixed, impossible to reduce to a hex code. Color bleeds at edges like pigment hitting wet paper. Every palette choice is painstaking — each hue selected through countless iterations by someone who has spent years understanding both screens and canvases.

## Scale and Rhythm
Macro and micro coexist. Sweeping arcs of color at monumental scale, then tiny precise labels — version numbers, coordinates, technical glyphs. The rhythm alternates: vast breathing zones, then dense concentrated nodes of information. This contrast is not accidental but the product of deep compositional expertise.

## Composition and Hierarchy
The eye moves in a specific choreography: drama first, then detail, then quiet. A single dominant visual gesture anchors the composition. Secondary elements orbit with gravitational precision. Every pixel placed with the deliberateness of someone who knows that one millimeter changes everything.

## Visual Language
The aesthetic is tech-luxury — the confidence of a terminal prompt married to the sensuality of oil on canvas. Typography is sparse, surgical, essential. The whole piece should feel like something that took weeks to resolve — meticulous, labored, irreducibly itself.
